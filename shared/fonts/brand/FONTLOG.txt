FONTLOG for the PartPriced font family

Basic Font Information
----------------------

PartPriced is the type family of PartPriced Tools: a grotesk sans and a mono for prices, part numbers and tables, made from open-source (SIL OFL) fonts. Tabular figures and a slashed zero by default, square dots on i and j in the mono.

Version 1.000. Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (OFL.txt).

Styles and their sources
------------------------

- PartPriced Sans: modified from Archivo (Copyright 2020 The Archivo Project Authors (https://github.com/Omnibus-Type/Archivo)).
    Axes: wdth fixed at 100, wght 300-900.
    Upstream alternates made the default: tnum, zero.
    The dots of i and j redrawn: square.
    ss01: Proportional figures.
    ss02: Plain zero.
    Renamed (OFL 1.1); kerning, marks and the other kept features as upstream.
- PartPriced Mono: modified from JetBrains Mono (Copyright 2020 The JetBrains Mono Project Authors (https://github.com/JetBrains/JetBrainsMono)).
    Axes: wght 400-800.
    Upstream alternates made the default: zero.
    The dots of i and j redrawn: square.
    ss01: Plain zero.
    ss02: Round dots.
    Renamed (OFL 1.1); kerning, marks and the other kept features as upstream.
